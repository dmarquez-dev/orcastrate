## Advisory findings

1. **`_UncoveredAtSchemaHead` for `PermissionMode` (`src/main/store/schema.ts:210-212`) still uses the
   empty-array-literal spelling the probe proved inert.** A fifth `PermissionMode` would not break the
   build the way a sixth `PodStatus` now does. Out of scope for T0's criteria — criterion 6 asks only
   for the pod-status guard — and correctly documented as such, but it is the same latent hole one
   enum over. *Candidate for the backlog at Gate 3.*
2. **`migration-3.test.ts:239` (`expect(after[3]).not.toBe(fixture.applied[2])`) is a weak assertion.**
   It would pass on any stamp differing from a hardcoded fixture value. The surrounding equality
   assertions at `:237-238` carry the actual proof, so this adds little.

## Implementer decisions the review examined rather than accepted

- **The criterion-6 guard spelling.** The task said to add "the exact analogue of
  `_UncoveredAtSchemaHead`". The implementer refused, on the grounds that the literal analogue would
  never fire, and substituted a generic-constraint spelling. The review verified both halves of that
  claim by probe. Judged met.
- **The sixth edited line in the tripwire file.** `migration-2.test.ts:399` was not in the task's
  enumerated set. The review judged it the same class as the lines that were, and confirmed the
  protected items show no diff. Judged met rather than blocking.
