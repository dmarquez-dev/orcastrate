## Advisory findings

The six self-reported assumptions all hold up. The reviewer judged `unmanaged`
carrying partly-managed residue as permitted (dropping `permissions.defaultMode`
would violate criterion 4's intent outright), `sha256` + `sizeBytes` over
`mtimeMs` as **correct and better** (content is what the user would lose), and the
three remaining silences as resolved in the direction the contract's own comments
point.

1. **`watch(null)`'s handle release is proven only indirectly.** The
   `'emits nothing after watch(null)'` test asserts `pushes === []`, which stays
   true even if the handle leaked, because `flush()` returns early once `target`
   is null. Leak-freedom of `release()` **is** proven by the dispose and
   second-scope tests, and `watch(null)` calls the identical method — only this
   call site lacks its own assertion. Two lines to close.
2. **Two saves inside the same millisecond silently overwrite the first backup.**
   `backupPathFor` is fully determined by `now` and `copyFileSync` clobbers.
   Criterion 6 is met as written, but in production a fast double-save loses the
   older backup. A collision-avoiding suffix, or `COPYFILE_EXCL` with a retry,
   removes the case. **Carry to Gate 3.**
3. **`unmanaged['permissions']` and `unmanaged['env']` hold a residue, not the
   verbatim parsed value.** Correct for round-tripping, but T7 must not render
   `unmanaged` as "the keys Helm doesn't touch" — a `permissions` entry there is a
   fragment, not the file's `permissions` block. Worth a line in what T7 builds.
4. **The watcher tests depend on `fs.watch` delivering within `settle(6)`.**
   Deterministic in two runs here, but `fs.watch` latency is platform- and
   CI-dependent, and slow delivery turns `toHaveLength(1)` into a flake rather
   than a failure. A bounded poll-until-push, still with no wall-clock sleep,
   would be sturdier.
5. **The fs-seam scan has two holes.** The regex would not catch
   `import * as fs from 'node:fs'` or a bare `from 'fs'`. Nothing currently does
   either; the scan is just narrower than the invariant it defends.
6. **`acknowledgeOwnWrite` can swallow one genuine external change.** If another
   writer lands between Helm's `rename` and its post-write snapshot, the watcher
   adopts those bytes as Helm's own and emits no push. Bounded — the save-time
   re-read still catches it, which is exactly the guarantee/courtesy split the
   contract draws. A note, not a defect.
7. **`src/main/helm` reaches `../ipc/errors`, which transitively pulls
   `src/main/pod` and `src/main/store`.** Verified electron-free, and the file
   documents why `IpcFailure` beat a parallel `HelmError`. The blast radius is
   wider than criterion 1's per-file scan measures. **Same finding as T2's A5 —
   this is a cross-cutting pattern, not a one-off.**
