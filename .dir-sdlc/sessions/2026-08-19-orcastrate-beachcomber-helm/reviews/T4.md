  implementers' standing instructions.

  implementer's numbers; the contract fixes only the names. 32px is coherent
  against the pre-existing `--oc-pod-bar-height: 38px` and matches the stated
  "chrome and sub-chrome" rationale, so no change is proposed. T9 may tune them
  once the strip is real.
