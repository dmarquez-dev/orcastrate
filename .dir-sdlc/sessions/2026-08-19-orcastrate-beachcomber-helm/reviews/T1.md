## Blocking finding

**B1. Criterion 12 is not met: `npm run typecheck` and `npm test` are both red at
this commit.** `src/main/index.ts:91`; `src/renderer/src/state/store.test.ts`;
`src/renderer/src/__tests__/failure-paths.e2e.test.tsx`. Also fails criterion
13's final clause.

**The reviewer's independent read of the cause: the red is fully explained by
other tasks' ownership, and no part of it is T1's to fix.** Each mechanism was
confirmed rather than accepted:

- The typecheck error is `TS2739` on the `registerIpcHandlers({...})` call site —
  the direct and *required* consequence of criterion 7 widening
  `IpcHandlerDeps`. That file is outside T1's globs.
- All 9 test failures are **completeness sweeps over `INVOKE_CHANNELS`**, not
  behavioural regressions. The store failure's diff is exactly the six new keys;
  the e2e failures ask each new channel for a renderer surface T6/T7 have not
  built. Every other test in both files passes.
- Nothing failing is in `tests/unit/ipc/**`, `src/preload/`, or `test-utils/**`.

Structural point: criterion 13 anticipated that widening `OrcastrateApi` breaks
its two *constructors* and assigned both to T1 — correctly. It did not anticipate
that criterion 7's widening of `IpcHandlerDeps` breaks its one *caller*. That is a
gap in the manifest, not in the implementation.

### Ranked remediation options, as the reviewer worded them

1. **Amend the manifest: accept the red window and make T8 the closing task, with
   the `src/main/index.ts` wiring as its first item.** The three-line change is
   constructing the two real services and passing them on `IpcHandlerDeps` —
   precisely T8's stated job, and `handlers.ts:71-77` already documents T8 as
   "the only place the real implementations are constructed". Criteria 12/13
   become T8's to satisfy. *Evidence: repo — `handlers.ts` docblock; T8's
   ownership of `src/main/index.ts`.*
2. **Amend T1's `owns` to add `src/main/index.ts` and have T1 wire a minimal deps
   pair.** Closes the typecheck window one task earlier, but T1 would have to
   construct services T2/T3 have not written, so it means a throwaway stub in
   production main — and it opens a write conflict with T8, which runs against
   the same file. *Evidence: judgment.*
3. **Do nothing structural; record criteria 12/13 as knowingly deferred to the
   session merge gate.** Cheapest, and defensible because the branch is not
   merged mid-session, but it leaves the manifest's per-task green invariant
   unstated for every later task that inherits the same red. *Evidence:
   judgment.*

Reviewer recommends option 1, and states that no revision round on T1 is
warranted — a revision round cannot fix this without a manifest amendment first.
