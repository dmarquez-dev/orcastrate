## Advisory findings

- **T2's "three places" count is imprecise as stated.** There are six `incoming: null`
  assignments, not three. The claim is right about *clearing events* and wrong as a literal
  occurrence count. Phrase it as "three clearing-event sites" if repeated downstream.
- **A fifth site nulls `incoming`, and it is fine.** `resetHelmForReopen`
  (`panel-store.ts:329-332`) replaces the whole Helm state via `newHelmState(null)` when the
  panel closes, so `incoming` goes null with `document` and `draft`. Not a targeted clearing
  event and not a departure from contract §2, but a future reader auditing "exactly four
  events" will find it.
- **T2's `actionError` advisory is correct, and the interaction is better than it argued.**
  `HelmNotices.tsx:87` renders the conflict banner from `conflict` alone, and `compare()`
  never writes `conflict`. `HelmNotices.tsx:56` suppresses the action-error line when it
  duplicates the conflict string, so after a `compare()` during a conflict the banner stands
  alone with no duplicate beneath it — the intended presentation.
- **The stale-count comment cluster is worse than T2 described, and still out of scope.**
  `panel-store.test.ts:80` says "The ten that answer with an `ActionResult`. The other two
  return `void`." At `c3d90cc` the real split was 6/6; it is now 7/6 after `compare()`. The
  docblock five lines above says "The three that are declared `void`" when
  `renderer-panels.ts` declares six. Leaving it alone was correct — it pins nothing — but a
  follow-up should correct the whole cluster at once rather than the one number.
