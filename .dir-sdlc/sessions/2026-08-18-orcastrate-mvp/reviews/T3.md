## Advisory findings

Carried to Gate 3. No options attached — reviewers attach options to blocking
findings only.

- **B1** — Unknown Pod/lane on `append`/`appendMany` maps to `CONFLICT`, not
  `NOT_FOUND`: `toEventStoreError` sends every `SQLITE_CONSTRAINT*` to
  `CONFLICT`, while `createLane` pre-checks the Pod specifically to yield
  `NOT_FOUND`. The module is internally inconsistent about the same condition.
  **T4, T5, and T7 branch on `code`** — they must be told.
- **B2** — `events.pod_id` and `events.lane_id` are independent foreign keys, so
  appending with Pod A and a lane belonging to Pod B persists and then surfaces
  in `readEvents({podId: A})`. A composite FK to `lanes(pod_id, id)` would close
  it. Outside the criteria.
- **B3** — A new `db.transaction()` wrapper is constructed on every `append`
  while prepared statements are cached. Performance only, irrelevant at MVP
  volumes.
- **B4** — The criterion-1 surface test uses `expect.arrayContaining`, which
  proves no contract method is missing but cannot catch an added one. The "none
  added" half rests on the `implements EventStore` typing, which is sufficient in
  practice since extras are unreachable through the factory's type.
- **B5** — Session note, not a T3 finding: `npm run typecheck` is red repo-wide
  on T2's `probe/run-probe.ts`. Re-run the gate once T2 lands.
