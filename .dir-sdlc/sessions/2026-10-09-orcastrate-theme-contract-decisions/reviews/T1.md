## Advisory findings

Recorded, no revision round. Carried to Gate 3 for a disposition.

1. **`Panels.styles.test.ts` does not guard the selector swap.** It imports
   `PanelBar.module.css?raw` but asserts only on `.panelBar`, so criterion 2's selector
   swap has no stylesheet-level guard against a silent revert to `[aria-pressed='true']`.
   The implementer flagged this itself; no criterion requires such a guard. A future
   session may want a raw-CSS assertion on the open-state selector.
   Group: *test and CI concerns*.
2. **`aria-current="false"` is emitted on every closed panel button** rather than the
   attribute being omitted. The reviewer did not spend a lookup, because no criterion
   turns on ARIA-spec conformance and criterion 1 is satisfied either way. Its stated
   belief — **as belief, not verified against a source in this session** — is that
   `false` is a valid `aria-current` token meaning "not the current item", so this is
   sound. Treat as unconfirmed. Group: *correctness risks*.
3. **Lane-toggle prose mentions `aria-pressed`.** `lanes/LaneRow.tsx:46` and
   `lanes/LaneRail.test.tsx:201` carry prose about `aria-pressed`, correctly left
   untouched: those are the genuine two-state lane toggles and lie outside T1's ownership
   set. Noted so a future `aria-pressed` sweep does not mistake them for stragglers.
   Group: *cosmetics*.
