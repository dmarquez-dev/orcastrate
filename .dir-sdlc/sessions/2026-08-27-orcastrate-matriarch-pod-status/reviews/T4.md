## Blocking finding

**The change breaks three passing tests outside the ownership set.** Repo-wide `vitest run`: **2 files failed | 131 passed**, **3 tests failed | 2086 passed** — independently confirmed by the orchestrator.

- `src/renderer/src/__tests__/pod-stop.e2e.test.tsx:476` — "turns the Matriarch row from Running to Stopped and freezes its duration across two advances of the clock"
- `src/renderer/src/__tests__/pod-stop.e2e.test.tsx:784` — "shows a Lane closed / Stopped row in conversation mode after the stop click, with no transcript re-read"
- `src/renderer/src/__tests__/relaunch.e2e.test.tsx:559` — "keeps the reconciled duration frozen while the Pod that kept its session goes on counting"

All three fail identically: `expected 'matriarch (Matriarch, pinned to top)N…' to contain 'Running'`. The rendered DOM now carries `data-pod-status="idle"` on the Matriarch and `data-status` only on the Calves, so the row reads **Idle** where these suites expect **Running**. **This is T4's intended behaviour meeting assertions written against the old vocabulary — not a defect in the row.**

Causation proved, not asserted: with T4's four modified files stashed and `pod-governed-row.test.tsx` moved aside, both suites are 16/16 green; restored, they are red. The worktree was restored byte-identically afterwards (`diff -q` SAME on all five files).

**No acceptance criterion covers this** — c14's verification set named only `lanes/`, `styles/` and `test-ids.test.tsx`. It is blocking under "the diff breaks something outside the task", and the fix touches files T4 does not own, so it is an orchestrator scheduling decision rather than a revision T4 can make alone.

### Ranked remediation options

1. **Update the three assertions to the Pod vocabulary, keeping a positive pre-state anchor.** `pod-stop.e2e.test.tsx:473-474` exists precisely so the test "could not tell 'the fix works' from 'the row never said Running'" — the replacement must assert the row reads a specific Pod word *before* the stop, not merely drop the check. `relaunch.e2e.test.tsx:501` (`not.toContain('Running')`) becomes weaker for free once no row says Running, so it needs the same treatment. *Evidence: `repo` — the cited comment states the falsifiability requirement.*
2. **Assert the attribute rather than the word** (`[data-pod-status]` / `[data-status]` plus its value) at those three sites. More stable against label changes, but drops the user-visible-text check these e2e suites are built on. *Evidence: `repo` — matches the pattern already at `stopped-lane.test.tsx:129` and `orphaned-lane.test.tsx:110`.*
3. **Reconsider whether the Pod branch should apply when the Pod is `idle`** — have the state layer hand down `null` outside active turns. This makes the e2e suites pass untouched but reintroduces exactly the ambiguity this session exists to remove, and contradicts c2. Listed for completeness; **not recommended**. *Evidence: `judgment`.*
