## Advisory findings

No options attached — `dir-reviewer` supplies remediation options for blocking findings only.

1. **The corrected prose states a boundary-sensitive count without saying how tight the
   boundary is.** `orphaned`/`running` computes **1.1949**, 0.005 inside the 1.2 line, and
   `done`/`stopped` sits just outside at **1.2062**. The reviewer confirms the implementer's
   volunteered observation — pre-`stopped` under 1.19 is exactly **five** — so the most
   probable origin of the original `five` is a 1.19-ish threshold rather than a wrong
   population, and the block as left does not say so. A future reader re-deriving at 1.19 gets
   five again with nothing in the block to explain it. The reviewer verified the diff does not
   quietly act on this: the correction states 7 and 6 at the 1.2 line only, with no 1.19 figure
   and no threshold change, so the implementer's claim that it did not let the observation
   adjust the correction is borne out. Criterion 6 unaffected — a boundary-sensitive count is
   not a false one. `tokens.css:88-91`.
2. **Pre-existing and untouched: an ambiguous referent.** The amendment prose reads "WCAG
   contrast 1.796 against `idle` and 3.903 against `closed`, so the semantically adjacent pair
   clears the shipped `closed`/`idle` precedent (2.173)". Only the 3.903 pair clears 2.173;
   1.796 does not. Both figures are correct as stated (reviewer verified both); this task did
   not write the sentence and no criterion reaches it. `tokens.css:79-82`.
3. **One read-only `cd` against the never-`cd` rule**, self-disclosed by the implementer, with
   every later command using `git -C`. Procedural, read-only, no write outside ownership.
