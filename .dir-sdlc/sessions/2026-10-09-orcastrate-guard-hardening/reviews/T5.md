## The criterion 7 ruling, in the reviewer's words

> The criterion is **self-contradictory as written**: it demands the visibility
> guards go red *"because producer and filter now read one const"* — but that
> premise is precisely what makes the conclusion unreachable. Once both sides
> re-export one binding, a visibility test that builds its event from that same
> const is invariant under any rename of it; producer and filter move together and
> the notice stays visible. **Satisfying criterion 7's literal observable would
> require criteria 2 and 3 to *not* hold.** An implementer cannot be failed for
> declining to satisfy a contradiction, and the demonstrated-red obligation is
> discharged by **real** red on three tests that genuinely can fail and did.
> Gate 2 already spotted this exact self-comparison trap for the reference pins
> (criterion 5) and simply did not propagate the insight to criterion 7's
> prediction.

Confirmed: `startup-reconciliation.test.tsx` (3 tests) and
`TranscriptPane.filter.test.tsx` (25 tests) both stayed **green** under the
rename. What the criterion was protecting — that a marker rename cannot silently
hide the notice — is discharged by strictly stronger means: the regression is no
longer merely detected but **inexpressible**.
