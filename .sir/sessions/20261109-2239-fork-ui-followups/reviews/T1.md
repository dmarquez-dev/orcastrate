### Blocking

### Advisory

- A1: a comment overstates what `flex: 1 1 0` does. `PodsChrome.module.css:32-33` says the row
  "takes only the width the Pod bar panel leaves over", and the test docstring at
  `fork-error-layout.styles.test.ts:15-17` repeats it. Both items have flex-grow 1, so the
  leftover space is shared. The wording should say the panel keeps its content basis.
- A2: the case where the tabs alone fill the row is unmeasured. There, the panel still gives up
  the alert row's min-content width (the button plus about one glyph). The implementer
  disclosed this, and no criterion covers it.

### Criterion questions

- C2 (raised as E3): "keeps the Pod bar at the width it has without an alert". Does this
  require the `[data-testid="pod-bar"]` element's own width to be unchanged, or is its purpose
  fully stated by "so each Pod tab title keeps its width"? Under the fix the bar's width
  changes when there is leftover space, and the title widths do not.
  1. Rule that the title-width clause is the requirement, and amend C2's wording to match.
     Rationale: the scored evidence C2 itself cites, O17, passes on title widths and only
     *records* the `pod-bar` width (contract `:106`). Evidence: repo
     (`contracts/fork-ui-driver-followups.md:106`).
  2. Keep the letter, and require the bar width to be unchanged, for example
     `.dismissible { flex: 0 1 0 }` (no grow). Rationale: the panel would absorb all leftover
     space, but the alert row would shrink to its min-content width (about one glyph per line
     under `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, clamped to 2 lines), so the visible message would be
     mostly unreadable. Evidence: judgment.

### Resolution

- E3, answered by the user: the title-width clause is the requirement. C2 was amended in
  `manifest.json` (A1, with no tasks in flight). Under the amended wording, C2 rests on what the
  reviewer found: the declarations are named and asserted, and title widths are kept. O17 in
  T2 is the scored evidence. T1 is committed as it stands. No revision round was needed.
- A1 and A2 are advisories. They are carried to Gate 3.
