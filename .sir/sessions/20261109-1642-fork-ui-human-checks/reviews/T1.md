Each one fails rather than passes when missing. Putting `tol` into each observation's `refs` would bring it under the null rule mechanically.

The reading is reasonable, but the wording is ambiguous, and T3 inherits it.
3. **Extra meta fields.** `meta.sandboxRemoved` and `meta.precondition` (driver:365-366) are additions beyond I2's shape. Nothing is renamed or dropped.
